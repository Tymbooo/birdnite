Vokamon privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Vokamon is a language-learning app for adults (Spanish, English and Dutch) built around TV, music and news. This policy explains what happens with your data. The short version: everything works without an account and stays on your device. If you choose to sign in, your progress is also stored on our server so you can use it on other devices. We never sell or share your personal data, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Without an account, your progress (levels, daily XP, quiz results, chosen language and theme, and settings) is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. We cannot see it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted.

Optional account (sign in with Google)

You can sign in with Google (“Save my progress” in the app's settings) to keep your progress and use it on other devices. Accounts are for people aged 16 and older (or with a parent's permission).

  • What we store when you sign in: your e-mail address (kept by the login system), a random account number, your quiz answers with the time you gave them, which episodes you unlocked or passed, and a few settings (level, native language, theme, age range).
  • What we do not store: your name, date of birth, location, contacts, or anything from Spotify, Last.fm or your phone's notifications.
  • Where: with our hosting provider Supabase, in an EU data centre (Ireland). Google tells us that you signed in and your e-mail address; Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.
  • Why: only to give you your progress on every device and to schedule which words to practise again. Not for advertising, and it is not shared with anyone.
  • Who can see it: only you, and us in order to run and support the app. Each account is separated from the others by database rules.
  • How long: until you delete your account.
  • Delete or export: in the app, open settings, then “Save my progress”, then “Delete my account and data”. That removes your login and everything stored for it. To get a copy of your data, e-mail us.

Your rights (access, correction, deletion, a copy of your data, and complaint to your data protection authority) apply as under EU law. Write to the contact address below.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Vokamon contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools. We do not use cookies for tracking.

Optional connections to other services

These only happen if you choose to use the feature. The app talks to these services directly from your device, and their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

  • This phone (Android app, optional): if you switch it on, Android asks you to give Vokamon notification access. The app then only reads which song your music app is playing (title and artist) and keeps a list of songs that played for at least 30 seconds, on your device, to award XP. It does not read the content of your notifications or messages, and it never sends this list anywhere. You can switch the access off at any time in Android settings.
  • Last.fm (music): if you enter your Last.fm username, the app asks Last.fm for your recently played tracks to build song quizzes. The username is stored only on your device.
  • Spotify (music): if you connect Spotify, you sign in on Spotify's own page. The access token Spotify returns is stored only on your device and used to read your recently played tracks.
  • YouTube (TV): after a quiz about an episode you can watch it in an embedded YouTube player (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube/Google may receive technical information such as your IP address when the player loads, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Links: some buttons open other websites (for example Spotify or Last.fm). That is governed by those sites.

Children

Vokamon is not directed at children under 13. Accounts are only for people aged 16 and older (or with a parent's permission); anyone younger can still use the app, with progress kept on the device only. If you believe a child has created an account without permission, tell us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can disconnect Last.fm or Spotify inside the app at any time, delete your account and its server data in the app, and delete everything stored on your device by clearing the app's data in your device settings or uninstalling it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
